Caribe Rhythm Hub

1. Site Name

Caribe Rhythm Hub — This name clearly identifies an interactive learning hub and instrument reference guide focused on Afro-Caribbean percussion, Salsa montuno grooves, and Merengue rhythms. It communicates cultural identity while remaining accessible to learners worldwide.

Optional domain availability: cariberhythmhub.org

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Caribe Rhythm Hub is to provide a structured educational resource and instrument guide for Latin percussionists. The website will feature instrument profiles (bongos, güira, tambora, and congas), rhythm breakdowns for Salsa and Merengue, skin-head tuning and care tips, and an interactive practice planner and workshop registration form.

3. Scenarios

  • What are the core percussion instruments in traditional Merengue and Salsa ensembles, and how do their rhythmic roles complement one another?
  • How do I tune and care for natural skin drumheads on bongos and tamboras in warm, humid climates?
  • How can I filter instruments by family or tempo, save favorite rhythm notes, and register for percussion practice sessions?

4. Color Schema

The color schema pairs deep coastal slate and warm parchment with terracotta accents inspired by handcrafted drums, maintaining high contrast for readability and accessibility.

Deep Teak Slate

#1E2D2F

Applied to header, footer, main headings, and primary navigation surfaces.

Warm Parchment

#FAF6F0

Applied as the main page background color for comfortable reading.

Terracotta Drum

#A63A1E

Applied to primary buttons, links, borders, and key visual accents.

Charcoal Ink

#222222

Applied to body text, lists, and labels for high contrast against light backgrounds.
